# Local assistant recovery — 13 September 2026

## Working now

The ERP chat has a server-owned, multi-turn order draft. It asks for missing
phone, quantity, exact catalog product and manufacturer, and explicitly asks
whether an amount such as `6` means 6 or 6,000 pounds. It does not silently
choose a similar supplier, assume a quantity, or multiply a quoted total as
though it were a unit price. Corrections and cancellation replace the pending
values. A signed preview and explicit employee approval still precede writes.

The user example was executed successfully in an isolated database, with a
6,000 cost / 12,000 selling price and the explicitly selected factory. No
business records were written before approval, duplicate execution made no
second order, and the catalog's default prices/manufacturer were unchanged.
The JSON-signature/persisted-preview mismatch that formerly prevented previews
was fixed and is covered by a JSON-roundtrip and real ledger regression.

The existing compiler exposes 14 allowlisted operation kinds, not unrestricted
database access. Phone-less leads open the new CRM form with an unsaved draft.
Receipts, expenses and other accounting work link to the accounting workspace;
the chat does not claim to execute its new ledger mutations. Optional browser
dictation may use the browser vendor's external speech-recognition service;
text review and a separate Send action are required.
